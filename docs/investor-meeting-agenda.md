## Talking-point cues per segment

- **2 · Problem & product** — Open on the shoebox → the AFTER timeline. Hit the moat slide hard: the *refusal*
  to score/predict/advise is the product and the legal safety. Worker owns the record start to finish.
- **3 · AI architecture** — This is the "is it real / is it safe" segment. Walk the brain, then the banned
  dictionary (violation, owes, liable, strong/weak case…), then the guardrails. Frame: restraint is engineered,
  not promised.
- **4 · Traction** — Be concrete and honest: product is LIVE, public demo at /demo, 91-firm CRM pipeline,
  founding pilot underway. Do **not** overclaim paying firms — the honest status is pre-pilot.
- **5 · Journey** — Emphasize the *gate discipline*: nothing opens until it's earned. Teleprompt answers the
  "personal touch" objection; Portable Record sets up the Phase 5 re-rating. Legal Fleet / Journey School are
  clearly-labeled separate ventures — vision, not a focus split.
- **6 · Economics** — Subscription, never per-lead/fee-split. Show the split and the ~66–72% margin. Note the
  three open items (Stripe products, commission basis, COGS estimate) if asked — don't volunteer as weaknesses.
- **7 · Org** — Founder-market fit (ran the method on her own claim). The one gap is engineering leadership;
  the four mandatory hires are the first use of the raise.
- **8 · The raise** — Lead with runway and what it proves (reach ~30–50 firms). Present valuation as execution
  scenarios, not a promise; if pressed on ARR/retention/CAC, the honest answer is "we launch [pilot date]."

## Be ready for these diligence questions (answer straight)

1. **"What's your ARR / retention / CAC right now?"** → Pre-pilot; first paying firms are the near-term target.
   The model is anchored to the funnel (~350 CELA firms, ~11% conversion, blended ~$420/mo).
2. **"Is the entity actually formed? EIN?"** → Slide 14 says "formed · EIN." **Confirm this is accurate before
   the meeting** — if it's still pending, say so plainly.
3. **"Founders' agreement and IP assignment — signed?"** → This is the honest open item. The deck already lists
   it as a prerequisite before outside capital. Have a date for getting it done.
4. **"Isn't this a lawyer referral / lead-gen play?"** → No — software subscription, worker-directed sharing,
   no fee-splitting. The participating-network is counsel-gated and dark until certified (§6155).
5. **"UPL risk?"** → The product refuses to score, predict, or advise — that refusal is the moat. AB 316 makes
   traceable, source-linked output a requirement, which is exactly the design.
