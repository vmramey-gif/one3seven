# one3seven — Phased Roadmap (Single Source of Truth)

**Created 2026-07-15. This supersedes all prior phase lists** (the shorthand and the longer doc). One
data asset — the organized, source-linked record — is the flywheel under every phase. Phases 1–2 are the
product you build and own; phases 3–4 are marketplaces/facilitation layered on top; phase 5 is expansion.

> **Discipline:** later phases don't relax earlier gates. "Marketplace next" ≠ "marketplace before it's
> earned." Each phase past 1 is a distinct build with its own gate and, for 3+, its own regulator.

---

## The sequence

### Phase 1 — Worker Intake Intelligence  ·  NOW
The organized, source-linked intake. Worker tells their story + uploads records → a dated, source-linked
timeline the attorney opens before the first call. Worker-first: the worker owns it and chooses their own
attorney. **Revenue:** flat firm subscription (Practice/Firm/Surge). **Gate:** ship the founding pilot; 3
paying CA employment firms.

### Phase 2 — Litigation Operations + Teleprompt  ·  the sticky layer (reinstated)
The intake becomes a **living case file**: transcripts, exhibits, deposition management, trial notebooks.
This is deliberately sequenced **before** the marketplace because it is the **highest-switching-cost**
product — it builds retention, which makes every later layer (marketplace, funding) more valuable.
**Revenue:** expanded subscription / seats — illustrative target **+ ~$249/mo add-on**. **No new regulator** —
it's internal firm workflow.

**Teleprompt (Superhuman Receptionist).** A real-time guided-call tool: during a live intake call, the AI
*whispers the next question* to a human coordinator on screen. The **AI never speaks to the worker** — a
human keeps the personal touch — and the call ends with a **completed, source-linked intake packet** (no
retyping). This is the direct answer to the "we want a personal touch" objection: keep the human, make them
superhuman. **Phase 2 trigger (gate to build):** 3 paying firms + **at least one "personal touch" objection
logged in the CRM** — the objection is the market signal that Teleprompt is wanted.

**Accessibility hotline (a Teleprompt delivery mode).** The same Teleprompt intake, offered **inbound by
phone** for people who can't self-serve online — **ADA/disabled, elderly, low-literacy, non-English, or
no-smartphone** callers. A **human answers**; the AI whispers non-leading questions and **never speaks to the
caller**; the call ends with a source-linked packet. **Guardrails:** fact-gathering only (never legal advice /
UPL), **two-party consent to record** (CA), feeds the **worker-owned** record (not a referral pool), and needs
E&O coverage for a vulnerable population. **Not pre-pilot** — needs staffing/mature AI; parked with Teleprompt.

### Phase 3 — Expert Marketplace
A vetted panel of expert witnesses & consultants (medical, vocational, economic/damages, forensic).
Because the record is already organized and source-linked, an expert is briefed in minutes, not hours.
**Revenue:** expert membership / marketplace fee — illustrative **~15% facilitation fee on the expert
engagement**, **never a per-referral fee on the legal matter** (anti-kickback line).
**⚠ Watch:** expert independence + anti-kickback structure. **Gate (do not skip):** 3–5 paying firms · an
expert-database head start · platform-capacity confidence.

**Digital Case Storage + Portable Worker Record.** Two paired assets: a **firm-side permanent case file**,
and a **worker-owned portable record** the worker can carry and share across firms. The portable record turns
a worker's organized file into a movable asset *they own* — deepening the moat and directly **supporting the
Phase 5 valuation re-rating** (the shift from "tool" to "network/platform"). Same organized-intake data,
compounding.

### Phase 4 — Funding Facilitation
Connect firms/matters to **third-party litigation funders**, informed by the organized-intake signal.
**HARD LINE:** one3seven is a **referral / facilitation layer — it NEVER becomes the lender.** Revenue is a
referral/facilitation fee, never interest or a lending spread.
**⚠ Regulatory (verify with counsel before any design work):**
- Third-party litigation funding is regulated **state-by-state; no uniform federal rule.**
- **N.D. Cal Local Rule 3-15** already requires disclosure of any non-party with a financial interest
  (including funders) — our home district.
- Rates commonly run **~15%, reported as high as 30%** — precisely why we stay the referrer, not the lender.
- **2025 TPLF statutes** passed in GA, OK, UT, AZ, CO, KS, MT, TN.
- **No confirmed CA-specific consumer legal-funding statute in hand** — a "verify with counsel" item, not
  something to build against from memory.

### Phase 5 — Expansion  ·  multi-state, then multi-category
Take the proven engine wider: **multi-state** (beyond CA — re-run the compliance analysis per state), then
**multi-category** (insurance / public adjusters, mold / toxic-tort — same DNA, validation-first).

## Parallel track — Participating-Firms Network  ·  cert-gated, not sequential
The "worker sends to participating firms" moat. This is **not a clean sequential phase** — it's a capability
that unlocks **only with State Bar lawyer-referral-service certification** (Bus. & Prof. Code §6155;
cf. *Jackson v. LegalMatch*). Once certified, it layers onto Phases 1–2. Until certified, it is **not built,
not pitched as live, and not framed as "leads" or "participating firms."** See [[project_founder_agreement]]
and the attorney brief.

---

## Hard lines (do not soften)
- **Funding:** referrer/facilitator, **never the lender.** (Phase 4)
- **Network:** only as a **certified** referral service. (Parallel track)
- **Marketplace:** membership/marketplace fee, **never per-referral.** (Phase 3, anti-kickback)
- **Everywhere:** organize-never-conclude; not legal advice; the professional keeps the judgment.

## The quiet moat across 3–4
The **organized-intake data** is what makes the marketplace fast (experts briefed from the record) *and*
what could inform funding underwriting signal. Same asset, compounding — that's the platform story.
