# one3seven — Social / Swag Copy Lines

Curated brand-voice one-liners for social posts, conference swag, shirts, and deck
kickers. House rule that makes these *work*: **the funniest lines aren't the edgiest —
they're the ones that reveal a truth employment lawyers see every day.** The humor lands
because it's *recognizable*, not because it's mean. Aim at the situation (chaos, HR
theater, corporate amnesia), never at the worker who got hurt.

All lines below pass the verb test (see `COPY_STYLE_GUIDE.md`): one3seven *organizes,
reflects, preserves, timestamps* — it never concludes, scores, predicts, or promises an
outcome. Keep it that way when adding new ones.

## Playful (kept deliberately clean)

Innuendo is OFF-LIMITS for this brand. Our users include harassment and discrimination
plaintiffs — words like *consent*, *boundaries*, and *the size of it* are the actual
subject matter of real cases, so a wink there reads as trivializing the harm. Keep this
tier to wordplay with zero sexual read.

- We believe in healthy attachments.  _(email-attachment / emotional-attachment pun — clean)_
- We like it organized before the first date.  _(dating metaphor, not sexual — okay)_

**Cut (do not use):** "Consent matters. So do timestamps." · "We respect boundaries.
Especially document ones." · "It's not the size of the file. It's the organization." ·
"We keep things organized. You make the moves." — reputational risk far outweighs the joke.
