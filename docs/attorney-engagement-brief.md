# one3seven — Fractional Startup Attorney: Engagement Brief

*A one-page brief to bring to (or send) a California startup attorney so the first meeting is fast and cheap.
This is the single highest-leverage action right now — one engagement unblocks four separate blockers.*

---

## Who we are (30 seconds)
**One3Seven Ventures LLC** — a California software company. Product (**one3seven**) is a SaaS platform that
organizes a worker's own employment records into an organized, source-linked intake for attorney review.
Principle: **"organize, never conclude"** — it does not give legal advice, score cases, or refer/select
attorneys. Pre-revenue; preparing for a first pilot and a small pre-seed raise. Two founders: Victoria Ramey
(CEO/builder) and Tadmor "Tad" Russell (Head of Sales).

## What we need (4 workstreams — priced together)
1. **Founders' agreement + IP assignment** (highest priority).
   - Papers a **50/50** split between Victoria and Tad, with **4-year vesting / 1-year cliff**, roles, and a
     CEO tie-break for deadlock.
   - **IP assignment** so the Company owns all code, the engine, the brand/name, and — critically —
     **work created before the entity was formed.**
   - We have plain-English **drafts ready** (`docs/founder-agreement-50-50-DRAFT.md`,
     `docs/ip-assignment-DRAFT.md`) — please review/finalize, don't start from scratch.
   - The founders are **life partners with children in California** — please handle community-property /
     spousal consent (and advise whether a pre/post-nup is warranted).

2. **Entity confirmation & clean-up.**
   - Confirm One3Seven Ventures LLC is **actually formed, in good standing, with an EIN.**
   - Advise on **LLC vs. converting to a Delaware C-corp** before the raise (SAFE round).
   - Confirm the **named insured** on our insurance matches the entity exactly.

3. **UPL / monetization sign-offs** (short written opinions we can keep in a data room).
   - Confirm our model stays clear of **fee-splitting (CA RPC 5.4)** and **lawyer-referral-service rules
     (§6155)** — we charge flat software subscriptions, never a % of any recovery, never a per-referral fee.
   - Green-light (or gate) the counsel-gated features: the **wage-exposure/damages estimate** and the future
     **participating-firm network** and **expert marketplace / funding facilitation** lines.
   - See `docs/monetization-map.md` for the full list of revenue lines and the bright lines we hold.

4. **Insurance + privacy review.**
   - Review our cyber/E&O quote (The Hartford) — especially the **retroactive date / prior-acts** issue and
     any **AI or legal-services exclusions** (questions drafted in `docs/insurance-questions-hartford.md` if
     created). Confirm the retro date can be backdated to actual operations start.
   - Confirm our **privacy posture** for sensitive CA worker data (CCPA/CPRA): privacy policy,
     data-processing terms, deletion mechanics, and breach-notification obligations.

## What we'll bring to the first meeting
- The two draft agreements above.
- `docs/monetization-map.md` and `docs/roadmap-phases.md` (business + phases).
- The insurance quote.
- Basic entity info (formation date, EIN if we have it).

## Budget expectation / ask
We're pre-revenue and cost-sensitive. We're looking for a **fractional/startup-friendly** arrangement — a
flat-fee package for items 1–2 and short written opinions for items 3–4, rather than open-ended hourly.
Please quote: (a) a flat fee to finalize the founder + IP docs, and (b) hourly or flat for the opinions.

## Where to find the right attorney
- **Referrals first** — ask other CA startup founders / your network for a startup-specialized attorney.
- **Startup-friendly firms / platforms** — many boutique CA startup firms offer flat-fee formation + founder
  packages; **Cooley GO** and **Clerky** provide standard templates a boutique attorney can finalize cheaply.
- **Fit checklist:** California-licensed, does **startup/emerging-company** work (not general business),
  comfortable with **legal-tech / UPL** questions, and will work **fractionally / flat-fee** at pre-seed.

*Bundling all four into one engagement is deliberate — the same attorney touches all of them, so do it once.*
