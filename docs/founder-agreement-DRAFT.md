The Founders are building one3seven, a software platform that organizes a worker's employment records into an
organized, source-linked intake for attorney review. This Agreement sets out ownership, roles, vesting, and
the key protections between the Founders. Detailed mechanics will live in the Company's Operating Agreement

## 3. Vesting (applies to BOTH Founders — do not skip)
All Founder equity is subject to vesting so that ownership is *earned over time*, not gifted on day one:

- **Vesting term:** 4 years.
- **Cliff:** 1 year — if a Founder leaves before the first anniversary, they vest **0%**.
- **After the cliff:** the remaining equity vests monthly over the following 36 months.
- **Unvested equity** is subject to repurchase/forfeiture to the Company (at the lower of cost or fair value)
  if a Founder departs.
- **Acceleration (optional, choose):** [none] / [single-trigger on acquisition] / [double-trigger on
  acquisition + involuntary termination]. Discuss with counsel.
- **Credit for prior work (optional):** the Founders may agree to treat some equity as already vested at
  signing to credit work done before the Effective Date. [Specify %, if any: ____ ]

- **Transfer restrictions:** a Founder may not sell or transfer equity without first offering it to the
  Company / other Founder (right of first refusal), and not at all before [1 year / an agreed date] without
  consent.
- **Good leaver / bad leaver** treatment: [define — e.g., termination for cause may affect repurchase price].
