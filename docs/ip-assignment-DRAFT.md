## 1. Assignment of intellectual property
The Founder hereby **irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title, and interest** in and to all
intellectual property they have created or will create that relates to the Company's business, including but
not limited to:

- Software, source code, algorithms, data models, and the document-organizing / extraction engine;
- The "organize, never conclude" methodology, guardrail/banned-vocabulary system, and prompt/AI designs;
- Product designs, UX, copy, brand assets, the one3seven name and marks, and domain names;
- Documentation, business plans, decks, financial models, and know-how;
- Any inventions, improvements, works of authorship, and trade secrets relating to the business.

("Company IP").

## 2. Moral rights & further assurances
The Founder waives any moral rights in the Company IP to the extent permitted by law and agrees to sign any
further documents (assignments, patent/trademark filings) the Company reasonably needs to perfect its
ownership.

## 3. Pre-formation and prior work (critical)
The assignment **covers work created before the Company's formation / before the Effective Date**, back to the
date each Founder began developing/contributing to one3seven: **Victoria — May 10, 2026; Tad — May 20, 2026.**
This closes the gap between when the product was actually built and when the entity was formed, so the Company
cleanly owns everything from the first day of work.

## 5. Third-party & open-source materials
The Founder represents that Company IP does not knowingly infringe third-party rights, and that any
open-source or third-party components used are identified and used in compliance with their licenses.
