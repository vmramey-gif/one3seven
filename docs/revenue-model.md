# one3seven — Revenue Model (Plain-English Money Math)

*Illustrative model. The adoption percentages (how many firms buy each extra, how many engine-rental partners
sign) are example assumptions to show the SHAPE of the money — not forecasts. Swap in real numbers once there
are paying firms. All lines are "rent the tool" money — nothing touches a case's winnings. Last updated
2026-07-16.*

See [monetization-map.md](monetization-map.md) for the full list of clean revenue lines and the bright lines.

---

## Building block — what one firm is worth
A firm on the **$549/month** plan pays **$6,588/year**. After the 20% rep commission and card/running costs,
**you keep ~$390/month (~$4,700/year)** from that one firm.

## Step 1 — Basic subscriptions only (today's product)
Blended **~$420/month** per firm (realistic plan mix):

| Firms paying | Per month | Per year | You keep (~70%) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | $4,200 | $50,400 | ~$35,000 |
| **40 (year-1 goal)** | $16,800 | $201,600 | ~$141,000 |
| 100 | $42,000 | $504,000 | ~$353,000 |

## Step 2 — Stacking the extras on the SAME 40 firms
| Money source | Example assumption | +/month | +/year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic subscriptions | 40 × $420 | $16,800 | $201,600 |
| "Rush" / priority add-on | 12 firms × +$100 | +$1,200 | +$14,400 |
| Wage-math premium (unlock the gated feature) | 16 firms × +$100 | +$1,600 | +$19,200 |
| Training / certification | averaged | +$1,000 | +$12,000 |
| Rent the engine to 2 software companies | 2 × $3,000 flat | +$6,000 | +$72,000 |
| **TOTAL** | | **~$26,600** | **~$319,200** |

**Same 40 firms + 2 partners → ~$319K/yr instead of ~$201K/yr — about 58% more, with no extra law firms.**

## Step 3 — The hidden giant: renting the engine (white-label / API)
Barely depends on law firms at all:

| Partners renting it | Each pays/month | Per year (this line only) |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | $3,000 | $72,000 |
| 5 | $4,000 | $240,000 |
| 10 | $5,000 | $600,000 |

**Ten software partners can out-earn 100 law firms** — cleaner and easier to service. This is the biggest
untapped multiplier.
