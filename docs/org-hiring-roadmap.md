# one3seven — Org & Hiring Roadmap

*Illustrative planning projection. Headcounts are ranges, not commitments — every hire is gated on its phase
opening and funding. Grounded in the current team and the 5-phase roadmap. Last updated 2026-07-16.*

---

## The hiring principle

1. **Hire when the phase funds it.** No headcount ahead of revenue — each role is added when its phase opens.
2. **Fractional before full-time.** Counsel, finance, and design start fractional/contract and convert to FTE
   as a phase justifies it.
3. **Retention is a hire, not a hope.** The 20% recurring commission is retention-tied, so Customer Success
   is a deliberate early hire.
4. **Officers follow the risk.** A new officer is added where the next phase concentrates risk — engineering
   first, then compliance, then finance.

> **⚠ The #1 gap today:** the team is sales-strong but the **founder is the sole builder.** A **founding /
> lead engineer** is the single most important near-term hire — Victoria cannot both scale the product
> through Phase 2 and run the company. This is the first dollar of the raise.
