Read first — vision vs. load-bearing constraints

How to use these numbers

The doctrine and the pricing structure are real and locked. The specific dollars are an ambitious, doctrine-clean target — deliberately ahead of the current product (no Stripe/metering/packs yet; that's the roadmap the model pulls toward). Take the vision freely. But four things bind even as a plan, because they're about aiming at the right customer, not timing:

Take as vision (don't sweat it)

  • Lanes / metering / Stripe — plumbing to build before billing; a sequencing note, not a flaw.
  • Element-packs, enterprise/SSO/SLA — roadmap the model pulls toward.
  • "Prices features that don't exist yet" — on purpose. This is the destination.

Hold as real constraints (4)

  • ICP aim: plan for referral-driven employment boutiques → lead with partner/paralegal hours + speed. The lead-gen story is a second ICP (firms that buy leads). Ask which they are first.
  • Wallet realism: small CA boutiques spend less than the ILTA benchmark — plan Conservative-first, climb as value is proven.
  • No intake-tool claim: we don't replace a firm's intake CRM. Keep it out of the pitch.
  • Savings = a milestone, not a claim: instrument minutes_saved, run one pilot, prove the hours before quoting them.
