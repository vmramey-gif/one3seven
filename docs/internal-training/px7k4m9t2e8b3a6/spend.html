The waste we target: lead-gen is paid per lead, whether it signs or not — averaging ~$284/lead and ~$468/signed case at a ~7–10% conversion. For a firm buying 100 leads, that's ~$25k in ad spend plus ~$30k of attorney/paralegal time reviewing ~90 declines. one3seven doesn't add to that bill — it makes the declines cheaper and faster, and raises the yield on leads already paid for. Claim: faster/cheaper no's. Never: more signings.
The one-line pitch this unlocks: “You already spend $40k / $400k / $1.8M a year generating leads. one3seven is a fraction of that line — and it makes the rest of it work harder.” We're not asking for new budget; we're asking for a sliver of the biggest budget they already have.
