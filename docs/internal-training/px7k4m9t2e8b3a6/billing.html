Billing & collections — annual prepaid

How one3seven invoices firms and collects annual-prepaid subscriptions — and where an AR tool like Biller Genie fits. The day-one rule: annual prepaid, collected by ACH.

The stack
one3seventhe plan→ Biller Genieinvoice · dunning · reconcile→ Processor / gatewayACH or card→ QuickBooksthe books

Biller Genie is an AR-automation layer, not a processor — it sends the invoice, chases it automatically, gives firms a pay portal, and reconciles to QuickBooks, riding on top of a processor. It complements Stripe; it doesn't replace the processor underneath.

Card quietly eats ~3% of a large annual invoice. Steer firms to ACH (they pay by AP/ACH anyway). Biller Genie also supports surcharging — passing the card fee to the firm — but ACH is simpler and cleaner.

Cost to one3seven & when to adopt

Fixed opex

Biller Genie ≈ $45–$135/mo (~$540–$1,620/yr), independent of firm count. Verify current pricing.

When it earns its keep

Overkill for the first 2–3 firms — send manual invoices, collect by ACH, free. Graduate in at ~8–10+ firms, when AR-chasing + bookkeeping eats founder time.

Verdict: Biller Genie fits the annual-prepaid model well — it's the AR layer you graduate into, not a day-one dependency. Day one: annual prepaid, ACH, a spreadsheet. Layer in Biller Genie for automated dunning + QuickBooks reconciliation once the invoice count justifies the flat opex.
