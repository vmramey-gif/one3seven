## ⚠️ Leilani — the structural flag

A silent investor requires things that do not exist yet:

1. **An entity to invest into.** The LLC formation is still pending. Money that moved before the
   entity existed is not an investment in the company — it is a personal obligation of the founder,
   and it must be cleaned up and papered retroactively.
2. **A security, properly issued.** SAFE, convertible note, or membership units. Even a friends-and-
   family investment is a **securities transaction** — it needs an exemption (Reg D) and any
   applicable California filing.
3. **A decision about entity form.** Most investors expect a **C-corp** for equity; an **LLC** issues
   membership units and K-1s and is awkward for future rounds. If more capital is coming, decide now
   whether to convert — it gets more expensive with every additional holder.
4. **Sequence.** Investors come *after* the founders' equity split and IP assignment are settled.
   Taking money into a company where two founders have no written agreement puts her capital, and
   the company, at risk.

**Action: confirm with counsel whether funds have already moved, and paper it correctly.**
