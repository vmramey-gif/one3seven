## 1. Purpose
The Founders are building one3seven, a software platform that organizes a worker's employment records into an
organized, source-linked intake for attorney review, and related worker-first products. This Agreement sets
ownership, roles, vesting, compensation, and the key protections between the Founders. Detailed mechanics live
in the Company's Operating Agreement, which controls in the event of any conflict.

## 2. Ownership split — 50 / 50
- **Victoria Ramey — 50%**
- **Tadmor Russell — 50%**

Equal owners. If the Company later reserves an option pool for employees/advisors, both Founders are diluted
**equally** so the 50/50 balance between them is preserved unless they agree otherwise in writing.

## 3. Vesting (applies to BOTH Founders)
All Founder equity is *earned over time*, not gifted on day one:

- **Vesting term:** 4 years.
- **Cliff:** 1 year — a Founder who leaves before their first-year cliff vests **0%**.
- **After the cliff:** remaining equity vests in equal monthly installments over the following 36 months.
- **Prior-work credit:**
  - **Victoria** is credited with **2 months** of prior vesting for building the product before the Effective
    Date. Her **vesting commencement date is May 22, 2026** (2 months before the Effective Date), so ~2/48 of
    her equity is vested at signing and her 1-year cliff is reached ~10 months after the Effective Date.
  - **Tad** — no prior-work credit; vesting commences on the Effective Date.
- **Acceleration:** **double-trigger** — unvested equity accelerates only if (a) the Company is acquired **and**
  (b) the Founder is terminated without cause or constructively terminated in connection with that acquisition.
- **Unvested equity** is subject to repurchase/forfeiture by the Company (at the lower of cost or fair value)
  if a Founder departs for any reason.

## 4. Roles & authority
- **Victoria Ramey — Founder & Chief Executive Officer (CEO):** product, technology, vision, fundraising.
- **Tadmor Russell — Co-Founder & Head of Sales / Business Development:** go-to-market, firm sales,
  partnerships, external communications.

**Decision-making (a 50/50 company needs this to be explicit):**
- Ordinary day-to-day decisions are made by the Founder responsible for that area.
- **Victoria, as CEO, holds the tie-breaking vote on ordinary business, product, and operational matters** to
  prevent deadlock.
- **Spend/debt threshold:** any single expenditure or new financial obligation **over $5,000** requires both
  Founders' approval. [Confirm the number with counsel.]
- **Major decisions require BOTH Founders' approval:** raising money, selling/merging or dissolving the
  Company, taking on significant debt, issuing new equity, changing either Founder's core role or compensation,
  or amending this Agreement.
- Unresolved deadlock on a major decision → Section 10 (including the buy-sell in 10(b)).

## 5. Time & commitment
- **Both Founders serve full-time.**
- Neither Founder will engage in a competing business without the other's written consent; separate, unrelated
  ventures must be disclosed in writing.

## 6. Founder compensation
- Both Founders are full-time. Because early cash is scarce, Founder pay switches on only when the Company can
  afford it:
  - Founder salaries are **$0 until the trigger below**, and do **not** accrue as a deferred liability before
    then (confirm treatment with an accountant).
  - Each Founder then receives an **equal** salary of **$84,000/year**, beginning at **the earlier of**:
    (a) the Company reaching **$30,000 in monthly recurring revenue**, (b) the Company **closing a financing**,
    or (c) **the Founders jointly agreeing in writing**.
  - This figure is a starting standard; the Founders will finalize the exact amount with their accountant once
    revenue is live. Salary changes are a "major decision" (Section 4), so either Founder can revisit it.
- As an LLC, "salary" is typically paid as equal guaranteed payments/draws; confirm structure and tax
  treatment with an accountant.

## 7. Intellectual property
Each Founder assigns to the Company **all** IP they created or create relating to the Company's business under
the separate **IP Assignment Agreement** signed concurrently (`ip-assignment-DRAFT.md`), covering pre-formation
work. **No third party besides the two Founders has contributed to one3seven**, and there are **no excluded
prior inventions** (each Founder's carve-out list is "None"). All Company IP is owned by the Company.

## 8. Confidentiality
Each Founder keeps the Company's non-public information confidential during and after their involvement.

## 9. Departure of a Founder
- A departing Founder keeps only their **vested** equity; unvested returns to the Company (Section 3).
- **Transfer restrictions:** neither Founder may sell/transfer equity without first offering it to the Company,
  then the other Founder (right of first refusal), and not at all for the **first year** without the other's
  consent.
- **For cause:** a Founder removed for cause (fraud, willful misconduct, material uncured breach) forfeits
  **unvested** equity but keeps **vested** equity; counsel to define "cause."
- **Death or disability:** on a Founder's death or long-term disability, vesting stops; the Founder (or their
  estate) keeps **vested** equity, and the Company/other Founder holds a right of first refusal to purchase it
  at fair value. [Standard treatment — confirm with counsel.]

## 10. Deadlock & dispute resolution
- **(a)** Any dispute or deadlock is first addressed by good-faith negotiation, then mediation, then binding
  arbitration in **San Joaquin County, California**, under California law.
- **(b) Deadlock buy-sell ("shotgun"):** if the Founders remain deadlocked on a major decision such that the
  Company's operations are materially impaired, either Founder may offer to buy the other's interest at a
  stated price per unit; the receiving Founder must then **either sell at that price or buy the offeror's
  interest at the same price**. This prevents permanent 50/50 paralysis. [Counsel to set timelines/mechanics.]

## 11. Drag-along / tag-along
- **Drag-along:** if a sale of the Company is approved as a major decision, both Founders will support it so a
  single Founder cannot block a bona-fide sale.
- **Tag-along:** if one Founder sells to a third party, the other may join on the same terms, pro rata.

## 12. Spousal / community-property acknowledgment
The Founders are life partners residing in California. Each Founder's spouse/partner (as applicable) signs a
**spousal consent** so the Company's equity is not clouded by community-property claims. Counsel to advise on
proper California treatment. *(Standard Company/Founder protection — not a statement about the relationship.)*

## 13. Entity note
The Company is a California LLC; ownership is in **membership units** (50% / 50%). If it converts to a Delaware
C-corporation for fundraising, this 50/50 split, the vesting terms (including Victoria's 2-month credit), and
these protections carry into stock and a stock purchase agreement. Counsel to align.
