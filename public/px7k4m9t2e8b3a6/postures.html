The greedy line — above this you lose the room

Firm > ~$450 / seat / mo Enterprise > ~$3,500 without real scope any % of recovery any per-signed-case fee

The first two breach the AI wallet or read as rent-extraction; the last two are barred outright. Past this line, price stops being ROI and starts being resentment — and churn eats the margin you reached for.

Recommendation: launch Medium. Reach the first ten firms with the Conservative number — but only through the capped, term-limited founding offer, so you get low-price logos without permanently anchoring low. Then climb toward the Ceiling as Coverage Rate and the correction-data flywheel prove the value. Never cross the greedy line.
