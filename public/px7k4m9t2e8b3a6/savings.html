Say it honestly
We free hours, not headcount — the time is redeployed to billable work and judgment, and we never recommend cutting staff. We do not reduce a firm's ad spend; we raise its yield. Every figure is an estimate that scales with intake volume — a low-volume or referral-only firm saves far less. The defensible claims are: fewer reconstruction hours, and cheaper/faster declines. The claim we never make: more signings or a guaranteed return.
Illustrative estimates for internal + counsel review — placeholders, verify. Labor figures use ~$65–70/hr loaded staff cost and conservative time-per-intake. one3seven organizes and cites; it never scores cases, promises outcomes, or gives legal advice.
