Become FL Certified

A required, self-paced course — about 20–25 hours over ~2 weeks. Four gated phases: foundations & doctrine, product & pitch, money & market, then a live certification. Your progress saves on this device. External certs are free.

🚫 Hard rule: you are not cleared to run a solo firm call until you're FL Certified. The doctrine gates are non-negotiable — a rep who can't hold the never-say lines is a legal liability, not just untrained.

Phase 2
The money & the market
~5 hrs

    The clean-money test + the money lines never to say.
  • Study — the Pricing Planning hub read
    Postures · example bills · what firms spend · what they save · billing. Read the "vision vs constraints" guardrails first.

Phase 3
Certify — live, with a founder
~4 hrs
  • Run a mock discovery call live
    Open without pitching, locate the friction, land Coverage Rate, ask the referral-vs-leads question.
  • Deliver the 15-minute demo live
    Hit the beats and the pauses; nail the source-link moment.
  • Handle 5 objections live — including the trap live
    Run the structure, hold the doctrine line under pressure.
  • Deliver the 60-second cold pitch live
    Hook · what · proof · ask — then silence.
  • Founder sign-off live
    A founder marks you FL Certified. You're now cleared for solo firm calls.

Not yet certified

Finish all four phases and the live sign-off. Until then, you shadow — you don't run solo firm calls.

Ongoing (after certification)

Attend the founder-led weekly 15-minute Intake Review. Re-certify quarterly — doctrine and pricing evolve, and the never-say lines must stay sharp. Optional baseline: Helsinki — Elements of AI (free cert).
